Beginner Trader Guide

This page is your step-by-step flow for using Trady safely and effectively. Focus on process, risk discipline, and consistency. The goal is not more trades, but better decisions.

Step 1: ContextStart from market context and volatility before signal hunting.
Step 2: ConsensusCheck agent agreement and confidence quality, not confidence only.
Step 3: RiskDefine stop-loss, position size, and max loss before entry.
Step 4: ReviewAfter execution, review outcomes and update your playbook.
Trading Workflow in Trady
01 · Market scan
Open Market Comparison page first
Identify which symbols are stable vs risky. Prioritize symbols with healthier stability and acceptable disagreement.
02 · Deep read
Move to Reporting page
Validate trend of drift/confidence, fallback rate, and alert transitions. Use raw event mode when you need event-level detail.
03 · Entry check
Open Decision page
Confirm final signal, confidence, and risk flags. Never take a signal without reading risk flags and disagreement context.
04 · Execution discipline
Plan trade size before clicking
Risk a small fixed percentage per trade. Example: 0.5% to 1.0% max account risk.
05 · Post-trade review
Export report and journal your decision
Save PDF/CSV from Reporting and note why you entered, what invalidates setup, and what you learned.
How to Read Key Metrics
Disagreement (Drift)

How often shadow and primary model disagree. low generally stable regime. rising means changing conditions.

Confidence

How sure the model is. High confidence is useful only if disagreement and fallback remain controlled.

Fallback Rate

How often the system uses fallback mode instead of full model output. High fallback means reduced model reliability context.

Alert Level

Normal/Warning/Critical from disagreement thresholds. Use warning and critical as risk-management signals, not entry confirmation.

Platform Best Practices
Use multi-page confirmation

Start with Comparison, validate with Reporting, execute from Decision.

Avoid overtrading

Set a max number of trades/day and stop after reaching daily risk cap.

Respect no-trade zones

If confidence drops and drift rises together, stand aside.

Prefer consistency over excitement

A stable, repeatable process beats random high-risk opportunities.

Common Beginner Mistakes
Ignoring risk flags

Mistake Entering only because confidence looks high.

Confusing confidence with certainty

Mistake All models can be wrong in new regimes.

No written plan

Mistake Trading without predefined stop-loss and invalidation level.

No review loop

Mistake Not learning from losses/wins through reports.
